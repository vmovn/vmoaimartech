# Product Direction

## Current baseline

- Product version line begins at **v1.0.0**.
- Technical seed/upstream baseline: **Swiffer 4.4.6**.
- Upstream releases are external engineering intelligence, not automatic upgrades.

## Product thesis

Vietnam-first operating platform for SOHO, solopreneurs and lean businesses.

The platform should help a small operator turn customer signals into repeatable execution:

`Capture → Identify → Understand → Score → Decide → Execute → Measure → Learn`

## Core capabilities to protect

- Organization/workspace multi-tenancy
- Customer/Contact identity and Customer 360
- Company, Lead, Deal and lifecycle data
- Activity/event timeline
- Omnichannel conversations
- Workflow/automation
- Segmentation and scoring
- API/Webhook integration
- AI/MCP/agent control layer

## Vietnam differentiation

Prioritize differentiation the global upstream is unlikely to optimize for:
- Vietnamese UX and domain terminology
- Zalo OA / ZBS / Zalo ecosystem
- Vietnamese phone/address/currency/time conventions
- Local sales/service workflows
- Local payments, accounting/invoice/tax integrations when validated
- Vietnam-oriented templates and onboarding

## Non-goals

- Do not compete with upstream by cloning every generic feature.
- Do not add integrations without a validated customer/job-to-be-done.
- Do not turn every SaaS dependency into a core dependency.
- Do not sacrifice tenant isolation, customer identity or update intelligence for short-term UI speed.
